About ICSLab

Research built for impact beyond the laboratory.

Founded in 2019 and led by Dr. Minhhuy Le, ICSLab is a key laboratory of Phenikaa University. We develop intelligent systems that combine sensing hardware, signal processing, and machine learning.

Our mission is to connect academic research to real applications through practical, reliable, and resource-efficient AI.

Research directions

Four connected fields. One sensing mission.

From physical inspection to edge intelligence, our work spans the full path from sensor data to trustworthy decisions.

01

Nondestructive Testing

Electromagnetic, ultrasonic, and visual inspection for safer structures.

  • Pulsed eddy current
  • Magnetic flux leakage
  • Visual anomaly detection
02

TinyAI

Efficient intelligence designed for microcontrollers and edge devices.

  • Embedded machine learning
  • Model compression
  • Real-time inference
03

Generative AI & LLMs

Language-driven systems for robotics, research, and automation.

  • AI agents and multi-agent systems
  • Human-in-the-loop automation
  • Industrial robot programming
04

Radar & Smart Sensing

Contactless perception for people, gestures, and vital signals.

  • Occupancy and people counting
  • Hand gesture recognition
  • Remote vital measurement
